Privacy Policy

Draft for the pre-launch website. Review and update this policy before collecting personal information or launching any provider integration.

Pre-launch notice: EarnHub is currently a prototype. Account registration, offer tracking, and payments are not active. Do not submit sensitive personal or payment information through this demo.

Information we may collect

When the service is developed, we may collect information needed to operate accounts, show eligible offers, prevent fraud, support users, and process rewards. This may include account details, technical/device information, offer completion records, and payment details required by a chosen payout provider.

How information may be used

Information may be used to operate the service, validate task completion, prevent abuse, calculate rewards, process withdrawals, respond to support requests, and comply with applicable legal requirements.

Third-party providers

If integrations are added, advertising, survey, offerwall, analytics, or payment providers may process data under their own terms and privacy policies. We will identify active providers and link to their policies before enabling those integrations.

Cookies and tracking

The live service may use essential cookies or similar technologies for security, session management, analytics, or provider attribution. Details will be updated before these features are enabled.

Data retention and security

We intend to retain information only as needed for the purposes described and apply reasonable safeguards. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to certain processing of your information. A contact method will be added before account features launch.

Children

The planned rewards service is not intended for children. Eligibility requirements will be stated in the final terms before launch.

Policy updates

This draft will be updated before the live service begins. The final policy will include an effective date and contact details.
