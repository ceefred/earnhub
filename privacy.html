Privacy Policy

Pre-launch notice: EarnHub is under development. Account registration and login are available for testing, but live earning offers, verified offer rewards, and real-money payouts are not yet operational. Do not submit sensitive payment information or cryptocurrency wallet secrets through the demo.

Information We Collect

EarnHub may collect information you provide when creating or using an account, including your email address and username. The service also stores account identifiers, wallet balances, reward records, and withdrawal request records associated with test or development activity.

Depending on the features introduced in the future, we may collect technical information, offer completion records, and payment details needed to provide rewards and process withdrawals.

How We Use Information

We use information to create and manage accounts, display account information, maintain wallet and transaction records, test platform functionality, prevent abuse, provide support, and prepare for future rewards and payment services.

Third-Party Services

EarnHub currently uses Supabase for authentication and database services. Information submitted through account features may be processed by Supabase to provide those services. Please review Supabase's Privacy Policy.

Future survey, advertising, offerwall, analytics, or payment providers may process information under their own privacy policies. We will update this policy before enabling those integrations.

Cookies and Tracking

Authentication and future integrations may use cookies, local storage, or similar technologies where necessary. Any additional tracking practices will be documented before the relevant features are enabled.

Data Retention and Security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for account management, testing, security, and applicable legal requirements. We apply safeguards intended to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. A dedicated privacy contact will be provided before the full service launches.

Children

EarnHub is not intended for children. Minimum age and eligibility requirements will be established in the final terms before the rewards service launches.

Policy Updates

This policy may be updated as EarnHub develops. We will publish revisions on this page and add an effective date before the live rewards service launches.
