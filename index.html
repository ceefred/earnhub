How rewards work

Built around transparency.

1. Complete

Read the task instructions and complete the advertiser's required action.

2. Verify

The provider reports a conversion to our secure backend. Browser-only claims are not trusted.

3. Credit

Eligible rewards are marked pending until validation and reversal rules are satisfied.

4. Withdraw

Withdrawals open only after supported payment methods, thresholds, and sufficient funds are configured.

We will not promise fixed earnings until real campaign payouts, availability by country, fees, reversals, and payment timing are confirmed.

EarnHub is a working name. This prototype is not a live earning service, financial product, or promise of income.
